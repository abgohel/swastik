About Us

Trusted Healthcare in Narol Since 2019

Swastik Medical & Dental Hospital is a well-established multispecialty hospital in Narol / Narolgam, Ahmedabad, known for ethical medical practice, compassionate care, and affordability.

With a 4.9★ Google rating and 600+ reviews, we have earned the trust of the community and currently serve 15,000–20,000 patients annually.

Our mission is to provide accessible, transparent, and high-quality healthcare, supported by modern infrastructure and experienced medical professionals.
